THE DETAILS

Open chess. Beautiful pieces.

Engine: Stockfish.js 19 Lite (GPLv3), running locally in a Web Worker. License.

Original brown board by the Lichess authors and pirouetti (AGPLv3+). Cburnett pieces by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPLv2+), from Lichess. Asset licenses.

Typeface: Google Sans, served by Google Fonts and bundled locally.

Puzzles are generated compositions. The Elo slider is an estimate based on material, competing checks and mating-piece complexity, rather than a measured player rating. M1 puzzles have exactly one legal mating move. M2 puzzles have exactly one forcing first move, with a finishing mate after every legal defense. Both modes are verified by Stockfish.
